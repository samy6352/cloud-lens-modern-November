## 6. Rules that bite

1. Never edit source while pytest runs (tests read/exec source).
2. Big builds: `CREATE TABLE AS`, not `INSERT … SELECT` (parallelism).
3. Benchmark real results, not `SELECT count(*) FROM (…)`.
4. Fixes must be generic (work through ingest/derived rebuild on live customer data), not tuned to the HDFC demo.
5. One commit per task, with the `Co-authored-by: Copilot` trailer.
6. The DB collation is `English_India.1252` (libc): text sorts/`lower()` are slow — group/sort on ints or
   `COLLATE "C"` columns; but a `COLLATE "C"` expression compared with a default-collation index column cannot use
   that index.
